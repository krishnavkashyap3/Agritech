Problem.

Small and marginal farmers in India routinely lose income to middleman markups, opaque pricing, delayed/unreliable payments, and lack of access to bulk buyers.
Existing platforms like eNAM improve this partially but still route trade through APMC mandis, leaving small farmers without direct market access.

Solution

Agri Tech connects farmers, bulk buyers, organizations, and individuals on a single platform where:

Small farmer listings are pooled into bulk lots automatically
Prices are set by a live AI-driven price-matching engine, not agents
Payments are protected via smart escrow (held until delivery confirmation)
Deliveries are coordinated through automated micro-logistics routing
Predictive ML models warn farmers of market gluts and recommend high-demand crops for upcoming planting cycles
Produce is tagged by lab-verified quality grade (organic / low-chemical / standard), with pricing that reflects quality tier

Core Features
🔐 Role-based sign-in for Farmers, Bulk Buyers/Organizations, and Individuals (Firebase Auth)
📦 Farmer product listing with quantity, location, and quality grade
🤝 Automated demand pooling of nearby small-quantity listings into bulk lots
📊 Live Market — Commodity View: real-time price, buy/sell volume, and trend chart per crop
🌐 Live Market — Overview (AI): all-commodity dashboard with AI-tagged demand/glut insights
💰 Smart escrow — payment held securely, auto-released on delivery confirmation
🚚 AI-optimized micro-logistics routing for multi-farmer pickups
🧪 Lab-tested quality certification (Organic / Low-Chemical / Standard) tied to pricing tier
🔮 Predictive ML model for regional glut warnings and crop recommendations
🌍 Multilingual, low-bandwidth-friendly UI for rural accessibility
